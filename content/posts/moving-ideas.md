I've been struggling with the idea of moving out a lot lately. I mean I knew being queer would be difficult, but jesus... I don't want to leave my family behind; I really don't, but despite that I have to be willing to. I think regardless of what the outcome will be when the plans are finalized I'll be more or less okay. It's just getting there that scares me.

First off there's living arrangements. I have someone I can live with for a while but I eventually wanna branch out and have something I can call my own. Living with them and their family would be a good idea though since I won't have to pay much for rent (if anything at all). Then there's finding a job, which I'm sure will be a bother despite my existing credentials and experience. I guess I'm needlessly worrying to some extent, but these are things that can go to shit in an instant if I'm not careful.

Another problem would be getting used to the new area and navigating my way through that new world. I'm less worried about that, since I can figure things out pretty quickly and build a relatively consistent routine. Hopefully my current thoughts become reality in a couple months time...
