As I get more and more keyboards, I realize how much I have a dependence on buying and using these keyboards for temporary happiness. I told myself before that the Dygma Defy would be my last keyboard, yet here I am eagerly waiting for a Moonlander and Voyager. At this point the only keyboard left there is to collect is the Advantage 360, and with how bulky and unwieldly it is, I can comfortably say I don't really want it; and I'm not even mentioning the fact that I ordered a VERY expensive custom-made Dactyl Manuform, which may take months to finish building due to my decision to wait for the seller to finish developing their tri-mode rendition (+$450 btw). I don't even think *Code to the Moon* or *If Coding Were Natural* have spent that much on keyboards, and the former has spent over 2k in comparison. I've easily gone over that with maybe about the same amount of boards. I'm truly a sick demented creature. 

Oh well at least I got my THOCC right?? THOCCCC
