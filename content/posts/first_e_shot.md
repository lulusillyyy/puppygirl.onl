This Tuesday I took my first estradiol enanthate shot and lowkey I was nervous as fuck doing it. I know doing things the first time can be nerve-racking, but I literally ended up SHAKING as I did the injection into my thigh. It's surprisingly easy and doesn't hurt very much though; I just need to do it a couple more times and it'll become muscle memory for me.

In other news, I think I've been feeling the effects of burnout lately. Almost every day has been some form of day-in-day-out work. I go to my day job, get home, spend a few hours trying (and half the time failing) to relax, get ready and go to my second job, and finally go home at midnight only to sleep around 1-2 in the morning; then I wake up at 8 to do it all over again. Like... don't get me wrong, I love getting paid every week and making more money, but at the same time I've cut down my free time for rest and recreation a lot in the process. Even though I try to do my own thing on the clock when I have free time, I still feel the whole "work" thing loomingover me. I don't feel very free. In some ways I feel more trapped than I did before. So far I'm managing, but I think as things progress and when my day job kicks into high gear, I won't be able to cope and have to quit. It's hard though, as I'm also providing for someone dear to me on the side, which eats up some of my funds. Sometimes I feel so boxed in I joke to myself that I may as well get a third job I can work remotely so I can maximize profits somehow. Honestly, like I said in my other post: fuck having two jobs. Don't do it if you don't want your soul sucked. The extra money may seem nice, but when you're living life waiting to have to leave for your shift, you'll realize it's nothing but another cage.
