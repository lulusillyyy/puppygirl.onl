On Thursday this week near the end of my therapy session I built up the courage to tell my therapist that I'm trans. It's really weird; I wrote down a bunch of poorly thought out hints and talked extremely vaguely, trying to beat around the bush -- which did not work entirely, mind you. After nervous deliberation I decided to write down the word "trans" on the top of the green construction paper in front of me. I had never felt more hesitant in my life honestly. They told me we'd talk more about this next week, but honestly I'm still unsure how to explain everything. There's so much about my current situation I'm gonna have to explain and I'm not sure where to start...

I'm so ready to move away man I know at the drop of a hat everything can fall apart and it worries me. Like -- what the fuck will I even do? How will I manage? Deadass got stressed out asf in the room and had to joke to play it down. I'm not sure if I even want them calling me by my real name. I've become so desensitized to people calling me my given name -- and even then butchering it constantly -- that I told my therapist they could refer to me the same as before, which I'm sure they're not going to take that for an answer. I'm just nervous at this point...
