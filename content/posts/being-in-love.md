It's hard being in love with someone when you were nothing but a failure to them in the past. Like. I dated this guy twice before already, and he's amazing in his own right. But I get so scared because of how much I hurt him with my own actions and misunderstandings of his intentions. I love being by his side but I was so unstable I couldn't make it work. I was too paranoid, too worrisome... I always mess my closest relationships up. Thankfully I'm starting therapy n shit this week so I can actually get some fuckin help. I've also been on anti-depressants as of late in order to help with my frequent anxiety and depressive episodes. Honestly I wouldn't be surprised if I end up having to take more medications depending on how mentally ill I sound. Either way I'm hoping for the best I guess...
