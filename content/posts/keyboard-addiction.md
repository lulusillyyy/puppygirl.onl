I have a serious addiction to ergonomic mechanical keyboards. I swear to god man I fucking bought a Dygma Defy for almost no reason. Well not really no reason, I did it cuz I wanted to try out a ZSA Moonlander but lowkirk I didn't buy it in time. It was literally $40 for like the keyboard itself without any switches or keycaps. I knew I shoulda bought that shit man FUCK. And I saw it while I was at work too the other night. Legit cucked myself. But oh well, a Dygma Defy is still really good for what it's worth, albeit much more expensive on average. $350 for a flashy keyboard that isn't contoured is kinda meh, but I'm interested how I can utilize the unique thumb cluster. Having 8 keys available to either thumb should allow for some interesting programming shenanigans. I'm glad they allow you to program up to 10 layers; that's much better than the Keyboardio Model 01's measly 5 layers through their configuration software. I will say that the Model 01's thumb cluster makes a bit more sense functionally, as it allows for all keys to be 100% reachable (assuming your hand fits the intended hand size range of the board).

Anyways tomorrow will be my third injection day so I'm kinda hyped about that I guess. I got therapy too, which I'm definitely looking forward to...
