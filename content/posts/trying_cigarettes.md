So a few weeks ago I had my first cigarette. Unfortunately I didn't record myself in a corny manner, as I was at work when I did it.

# Backstory
Honestly all that happened was I found a pack of Newports hidden in the guard booth at one of the spots I'm frequently posted at by the security company I work for as a second job. The moment I saw it I began to think about what it'd be like to smoke a cig. I didn't have a lighter on hand, so I was forced to wait until I got off my shift to get one. Unfortunately, I got cold feet and decided against it since one of my friends was trying to urge me not to go through with it. The night after, however, I decided I'd take the chance and purchase a cheap lighter from the gas station on my way home from work. That weekend I would smoke my first cigarette while I was on the job. 

# The Experience Itself

Honestly not much to write home about. Something tells me I have some sort of natural tolerance to nicotine, as I barely felt anything when I smoked my first cig. I found it quite amusing, considering everything I'd heard prior about how addicting cigarettes are (which is still true, don't use my experience as advice kids). The taste of menthol lingered for a few hours, but I became tolerant to it very quickly - especially after smoking a few more times on subsequent shifts.
