With my crippling keyboard addiction taking hold of my very being over the past year and change, I've found typing (and by extension writing) more enjoyable than before. In some ways it's almost therapeutic if I'm being completely honest.

When I was younger I genuinely wanted to become a world-renown fantasy writer -- mainly similar to the likes of Rick Riordan, J.K. Rowling and C.S. Lewis. That is to say, that dream *clearly* never came to fruition. But even as I grow older, I feel that hunger to write creep up on me at times. In a way, my writings here and on my main site act as casual outlets for my urges.

As of late, however, I've found myself wanting to write actual stories -- albeit my ideas are primarily relistic fiction pieces that revolve around my identity, my regrets, hopes and dreams, etc... I've already started work on the lore for my borzoi OC, as well as brainstorming ideas/drafting short stories I had brewing in my head randomly. I'm likely to either publish it here or somewhere else under one of the many domains I pay for for no reason. Whichever it is, keep an eye out.

*In other news, I'm still working on my next blog post, which will be about the Ergodox. Right now my main blog is primarily centered around keyboards as it's my current hyperfixation. In other other news, today marks my fourth estradiol injection. Next week will make it a full month! Huzzah!*
