Today has been another rollercoaster. I feel like everything went down this afternoon and led to this point where I feel nothing but utter numbness. I don't have any real reason to complain but I inexplicably feel this way -- it's debilitating. I feel like my boyfriend is going to break up with me any day now. I feel like I'm drifting further away from my own family and friends and there's nothing I can do about it. I feel trapped a lot. Like there's nothing left in this world for me. Like I don't fit in it; I'm a misshapen puzzle piece that's trying way too hard to be like the other pieces, all of which fit perfectly and serve their purpose.

Therapy recently revealed that I seemingly suffer with severe PTSD, which is surely a *fun* revelation. The less surprising semi-diagnoses' are moderate depression and moderate anxiety -- the point in general being that I'm quite fucked in the head for one reason or another. Oh and I also seem to suffer from occupational burnout, which checks out considering the fact that I can never get to work on time no matter what I do, and I don't find much joy in going to work anymore. I hate my jobs for the most part. The people are fine but I can't stand actually having to clock in and work. It's a waste of my brain I suppose... but I simply want to enjoy stuff. My productivity is at an all-time low. I can barely function doing anything. It's so fucking stressful and I don't know how to fix it. Maybe I'm supposed to wither away or something. I don't know anymore...

**Had a slight mental break in the middle of typing this so gonna end it here.**
